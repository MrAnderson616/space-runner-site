Privacy Policy — Space Runner: Corridor

Effective date: October 25, 2026

Space Runner: Corridor ("the app") is published by Nathaniel Anderson (AndersonsApps). This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: the app does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

What the app stores on your device

The app saves your game progress on your device only: best score, lifetime statistics (runs, kills, near misses, commanders defeated), which ships you have unlocked, and your settings (sound, haptics, fire button side). This data is kept in the app's local storage, never leaves your device through the app, and is deleted when you delete the app.

Game Center

If you choose to sign in to Game Center, the app submits your run scores to Apple's Game Center leaderboards and reports achievement progress. This is handled entirely by Apple's Game Center service under Apple's privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The app never receives your Apple ID, email, or any identifying information from Game Center. Game Center is optional; the app is fully playable without it.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • No accounts, sign-ups, or passwords.
  • No network connections of its own; the only network use is Apple's Game Center.
  • No access to contacts, photos, location, camera, or microphone.
  • No data sold or shared with third parties.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same address with a new effective date.
